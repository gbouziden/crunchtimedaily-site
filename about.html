About

Crunch Time Daily is a free daily basketball game. Every day there's one real NBA ending, the same for everyone. You make the calls, see what the real coach did, and find out whether you'd have done better.

It's built by a fan who wanted to argue with late-game coaching decisions with more than vibes. There are no accounts and no ads in the iPhone app, and your stats stay on your device.

Questions or a call you think was graded wrong? Email george@trychartread.com.
