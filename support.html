How do I play?

Every day there is one real NBA ending. Make the call on each possession, see what the real coach did, and get graded on your decisions.

Why didn't my streak count?

Streaks count consecutive days you finish the daily, by your device's local date. Stats are stored on your device, so a different browser or phone starts fresh.

How do I get on the leaderboard?

Open Stats, set a nickname, and finish 3 dailies within 30 days.
